Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by WAYFORTH (WAYFORTH Build, WAYFORTH Trucking and WAYFORTH Media). If you have any question about your information, email Support@wayforth.ltd.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms when you ask for a quote, a callback, or to work with us:

  • Your name, phone / WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Company name, website or channel link, if you add them.
  • Project or shipment details: materials and quantities, plot size, pickup and delivery areas, vehicle or load details, dates and any notes you write.

We do not ask for payment card details, passwords or ID documents on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, prepare your quote and follow up with you.
  • To arrange the service you asked for (construction materials, transport, or media work).

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How it is sent and kept

When you press send, your request travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and is delivered as an email to the WAYFORTH team. The website itself does not keep a database of submissions. We keep your emails only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal or accounting duties, then delete them.

Access is limited to the WAYFORTH team members who handle your request.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics profiles. It does not store anything in your browser to follow you around.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel hosts the website and processes standard technical data (such as IP address) to deliver pages securely.
  • Resend delivers form submissions to our inbox by email.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts; your browser requests them from Google.
  • NHTSA vPIC (US Department of Transportation) supplies the list of vehicle makes and models in the Ship a Car form. Only the year, make and vehicle type are sent, never your personal details.
  • WhatsApp opens only if you tap a WhatsApp link; its own privacy policy then applies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information you sent us at any time by emailing Support@wayforth.ltd. You can also ask us to stop contacting you.

Children

Our services are for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
